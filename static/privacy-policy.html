Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At Wechat (accessible from our web platform), user privacy and platform safety are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected, recorded, and processed when you use our real-time messaging services.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection to respect user anonymity while ensuring community compliance:

  • Display Names: When joining the chat room, users set a temporary display name. We do not require real names, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • Chat & Audio Messages: Messages sent in public and private rooms, as well as temporary voice notes, are transmitted via WebSockets to enable real-time delivery.
  • Local Storage Data: We store minimal structural data locally on your device browser (e.g., your display name, voice message quota tracking, and temporary suspension states).

2. Content Moderation & Abuse Prevention

To keep our community safe and compliant with global safety standards, Wechat employs an automated moderation filter:

  • Messages are automatically scanned in real time for abusive, vulgar, or profane language across multiple languages and transliterated scripts.
  • If violative content is identified, an automated first warning is issued. Repeated violations result in an automated 24-hour access restriction (Ban).
  • Suspension data is stored locally via secure client parameters to maintain community safety.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense

Wechat works with third-party vendors, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements on our web application:

  • Google uses cookies (including the DART cookie) to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.
  • Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their respective advertisements to measure campaign performance. Wechat has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We respect user control over their data. Users are provided with options to delete their own sent messages directly within the chat session. Temporary voice messages automatically expire after their session limit and are purged from storage.

5. Children's Information

Wechat does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided such information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such records.
