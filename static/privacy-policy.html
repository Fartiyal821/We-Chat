Privacy Policy & Legal Terms

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Welcome to Wechat. This document outlines our Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Community Moderation Rules, and Contact Information.

1. Information We Collect

We respect user anonymity and keep data collection minimal:

  • Display Names: Temporary display names are used during chat sessions. We do not require passwords or personal registration.
  • Chat & Audio Messages: Text and voice messages are routed via WebSocket services to enable real-time delivery across public and private channels.
  • Local Storage: Browser local storage tracks active username preferences, voice daily limits, and temporary restriction counters.

2. Abusive Conduct & Strict Ban Policy

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy against hate speech, harassment, profanity, and abusive content in all languages and scripts:

  • Automated Scanning: All public text messages and transliterated audio scripts are processed using deep content inspection.
  • First Violation: An immediate automated system warning is displayed to the user.
  • Second Violation: An immediate 24-hour access ban is enforced on the user's client, disabling messaging functions.

3. Advertising & Google AdSense Cookies

We work with third-party vendors, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements on our site:

  • Google uses cookies (such as the DART cookie) to serve ads based on users' interactions on this and other websites.
  • Users can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

4. User Control & Data Expiry

Users can delete their own active text messages for all room participants. Temporary voice messages automatically expire and are cleared after their session window.

5. Terms & Community Guidelines

By accessing Wechat, you agree not to post spam, unauthorized commercial communications, or illegal material. We reserve the right to suspend platform access for non-compliant users.
